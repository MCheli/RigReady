RigReady is a Windows desktop app for flight and racing sim rigs. Before a session it checks that the rig is ready for the setup you are about to use: the right controllers plugged in, the helper apps running, the monitors arranged, the default audio devices set and the game's files in place. It fixes what it can and launches the game, and afterwards it puts the desk back. Around that it keeps bindings and settings safe with backups, shows what every control does, and has tools for troubleshooting hardware. It is free and open source, for Windows 10 and 11.

The manual is in sections, in the order you are likely to need them: [installing and the first setup](getting-started/index.md); [the Play screen](play/index.md) you use before and after a session; [starting a session without the window](one-click/index.md); [setups](setups/index.md) and how to edit them; [monitors and audio](monitors-and-audio/index.md); [DCS World](dcs/index.md); [the other games](games/index.md); [what the buttons do](controls/index.md), with the cheat sheets and the trainer, for any game; [backups, sharing and safety](protect/index.md); [the hardware tools](hardware/index.md); [the app and its data folder](app/index.md); and [troubleshooting](troubleshooting/index.md).

A few features are not yet verified in the real game or on real hardware: they passed their tests on recorded data from one rig. The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) lists them under known limitations, and each page concerned says so.
