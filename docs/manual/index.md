RigReady is a Windows desktop app for flight and racing sim rigs. Before a session it checks that the rig is ready for the setup you are about to use: the right controllers plugged in, the helper apps running, the monitors arranged, the default audio devices set and the game's files in place. It fixes what it can and launches the game, and afterwards it puts the desk back. Around that it keeps bindings and settings safe with backups, shows what every control does, and has tools for troubleshooting hardware. It is free and open source, for Windows 10 and 11.

The manual is in sections, in the order you are likely to need them: installing and the first setup; the Play screen you use before and after a session; starting a session with one click; setups and how to edit them; monitors and audio; DCS World; the other games; backups and sharing; the hardware tools; the app itself; and troubleshooting.

Some features have passed their tests only on recorded data from one rig and not yet on the real thing. The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) lists them under known limitations.
