**Less time on rig chores. More time flying and racing.** RigReady is a free Windows app for flight and racing sim rigs. It does the work around the game: setting up your controls and screens, learning what every button does, getting everything running before you launch, and putting your desk back after. Every change it makes outside its own folder is backed up first and can be undone. It is open source, for Windows 10 and 11.

The manual is in sections, in the order you are likely to need them: [installing and the first setup](getting-started/index.md); [the Play screen](play/index.md) you use before and after a session; [starting a session without the window](one-click/index.md); [setups](setups/index.md) and how to edit them; [monitors and audio](monitors-and-audio/index.md); [DCS World](dcs/index.md); [the other games](games/index.md); [what the buttons do](controls/index.md), with the cheat sheets and the trainer, for any game; [backups, sharing and safety](protect/index.md); [the hardware tools](hardware/index.md); [the app and its data folder](app/index.md); [AI assistants](ai/index.md), which are optional; and [troubleshooting](troubleshooting/index.md).

A few features are not yet verified in the real game or on real hardware: they passed their tests on recorded data from one rig. The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) lists them under known limitations, and each page concerned says so.
