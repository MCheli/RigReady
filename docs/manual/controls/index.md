This section covers the **Controls** part of the menu under **Configure**: what each button, hat and axis does in the game. It works for every game whose bindings RigReady reads: DCS World and four racing games, iRacing, Le Mans Ultimate, BeamNG.drive and Assetto Corsa.

- **Bindings** has a game picker above the page, which lists the games found on this PC. With **DCS** chosen it is [DCS bindings](../dcs/bindings.md), with editing, cleanup and device ID repair; with **iRacing** chosen it is [iRacing bindings](../games/iracing-bindings.md), where an action can be bound to a button or a key. On a PC where neither is found, Bindings says so and points to **Games**, where a game installed in a place RigReady did not look can be given its folder. The other racing games show their controllers and bindings on their own pages under **Configure > Games > Racing**; see [the other games](../games/index.md).
- **Binding guide** is for DCS aircraft, so it is in the menu only when DCS World is found on this PC. See [Binding guide and AI help](../dcs/binding-guide-and-ai.md).
- [Cheat sheets](cheat-sheets.md): a drawing of each device with what every control does in the aircraft or car you choose, Quick look, your own photos, and Print or PDF.
- [Learn your controls](trainer.md): the trainer names an action, you press its control on the device, and what you miss comes back until you have it.
- **Overlay** shows each press over the game with what it does there; see [The overlay](../play/overlay.md).

[Kneeboard pages](../dcs/kneeboard.md), cheat sheets written into DCS's own kneeboard, are in the DCS World section.
