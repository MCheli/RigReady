# Code of conduct

RigReady is a side project for people who fly and race on a PC. Its issues and pull requests should be a place where someone with a first joystick and someone who has built cockpits for years both feel welcome.

## What is expected

- Be kind and patient. Everyone was new to this once.
- Talk about the report, the code or the hardware, not about the person.
- Say what you saw and what you tried. Disagree with reasons.
- Accept that the maintainer may say no. This is one person's side project.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, personal attacks, harassment, and sexual language or attention of any kind.
- Remarks that put someone down for who they are (their sex, gender, orientation, disability, age, race, religion or nationality) or for how much they know.
- Publishing someone else's private details: a name or address they did not share, or the serial numbers and paths from their PC.
- Spam and advertising.

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, reviews and commits) and anywhere someone speaks for the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here breaks these rules, use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment (the three dots at its top right), or email the maintainer, Mark Cheli, at <mpcheli7@gmail.com>. A report is kept private.

The maintainer may edit or remove a comment, close or lock a thread, or block an account, and says why when that helps.
