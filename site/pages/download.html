Download RigReady

Free and open source, for Windows 10 and 11.

{{RELEASE}}

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • Installs for your account only. It never asks for administrator rights.
  • The installer is not code-signed yet, so the first time it starts, Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (Microsoft Defender SmartScreen, publisher unknown). Choose More info, then Run anyway. Your browser may also ask whether to keep the file. Updates the app downloads itself install without either question.
  • Updates itself from GitHub Releases, on the stable or the beta channel (Settings, Updates). Never while a game is running.
  • Uninstalling keeps your setups and backups unless you choose to remove them.

New to it? Getting started covers the installer, the first run and the first setup.
