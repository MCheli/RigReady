Free and open source, for Windows 10 and 11.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • Installs for your account only. It never asks for administrator rights.
  • The installer is code-signed with the publisher name "Mark Cheli", through Microsoft's Artifact Signing service. Until it has earned a reputation with Microsoft, Windows may still show "Windows protected your PC" (Microsoft Defender SmartScreen) the first time it starts: choose More info, check that the publisher is Mark Cheli, then Run anyway. A RigReady 2 installer that names no publisher, or another one, is not from this page: do not run it. Your browser may also ask whether to keep the file. Updates the app downloads itself install without either question.
  • You can check the installer yourself: its Properties, Digital Signatures, name Mark Cheli, every release lists its SHA-512 in latest.yml, and the manual shows the PowerShell lines that compare it. The app checks the same value and the signature before it installs an update.
  • Only the update check and AI requests you send with your own key leave the PC. No account, no telemetry. What it writes, reads and runs.
  • Updates itself from GitHub Releases, on the stable or the beta channel (Settings, Updates). Never while a game is running.
  • Uninstalling keeps your setups and backups unless you choose to remove them.

New to it? Getting started covers the installer, the first run and the first setup.
